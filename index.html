🔐 Safety Tips

Stay safe while trading on GameVault

1

Never Share Personal Information

Don't share your passwords, payment details, or personal information outside the GameVault platform. Our system never asks for sensitive data via email or chat.

2

Use the Escrow System

Always complete transactions through our secure escrow system. Never send direct payments or trade outside the platform - you lose protection.

3

Check Seller Ratings

Review seller feedback, completion rates, and account age before making a purchase. Trusted sellers have badges and high ratings.

4

Report Suspicious Activity

If something seems off, report it immediately. Our team investigates all reports and takes swift action against fraudulent users.

5

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your account with 2FA. This prevents unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.

6

Verify Item Details

Carefully check item descriptions, screenshots, and specifications before purchasing. Ask sellers questions if anything is unclear.
